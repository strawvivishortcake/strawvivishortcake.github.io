Supplai addresses a fundamental infrastructure failure in global humanitarian medicine: frontline clinics in active conflict zones run out of insulin, antibiotics, and surgical supplies not because donors won't give, but because no system exists to connect a willing donor to a specific, verified, urgent need in real time. Billions in annual humanitarian aid flow through opaque organizations with high overhead and near-zero last-mile visibility, leaving clinics across Gaza, Sudan, Yemen, the DRC, Ukraine, and Myanmar chronically undersupplied. Supplai is a nonprofit technology platform that bridges this gap directly by enabling vetted clinics to post real-time, item-level supply requests that donors and NGOs can fund with full traceability. Every contribution is tied to a specific item at a specific clinic, with delivery confirmed via timestamped photo and GPS verification before funds are released from escrow. The result is a system where every dollar is accountable, every delivery is documented, and the global humanitarian aid ecosystem finally has an infrastructure layer built for the last mile.
