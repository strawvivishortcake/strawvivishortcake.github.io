I'm an undergraduate studying computational biology with research interests in

- Population genomics and health equity: I am particularly passionate about understanding how genetic ancestry and population-level variation contribute to disparities in drug safety, disease outcomes, and access to care, and developing frameworks that ensure underrepresented groups are equitably reflected in clinical and genomic research.
  
- Genomic basis of complex disease: I am deeply interested in uncovering the underlying genetic architecture of complex diseases such as cardiovascular conditions and cancer, particularly in identifying subgroup-specific risk variants and molecular mechanisms that drive disease pathology.
  
- Computational biology and multi-omics data integration: In the long run, I would like to further explore the integration of spatial transcriptomic, pharmacogenomic, and clinical datasets to build high-resolution disease models that bridge molecular-scale findings with clinically actionable insights.

I'm always open to new opportunities and connecting with other students and professionals. Whether you have a question or just want to say hi, feel free to reach out!
